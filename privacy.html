Information you give us

When you start a case, message us, or call us, you may share:

  • Contact details, such as your name, phone number, email address, preferred language and how you'd like us to reach you.
  • Details about your accident and injuries, including medical treatment, time missed from work and medical bills.
  • Documents and photos you upload, such as police reports, medical records, insurance information and photos of the scene or your injuries.
  • Messages you send us through this website, WhatsApp, text, email or phone.
