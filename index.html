Start your case

Three steps. No waiting room.

1. Tell us what happened.

Answer a short set of questions online or on WhatsApp, at your own pace. Upload what you have: photos, police report, medical bills. If something is missing, we'll ask.

2. We build your case.

Our software organizes your records and drafts the paperwork. A licensed attorney reviews your case, approves every document sent on your behalf, and makes every legal decision.

3. We pursue your recovery.

Your attorney negotiates with the insurance company. You see every offer and what you would keep on your phone. Your attorney talks with you before you decide on any settlement.

The first offer is often the lowest.

Insurance companies often make a quick, low offer before you talk to a lawyer. Some ask you to sign a release. Once you sign, you usually can't ask for more, even if your injury turns out to be worse.

Get your offer reviewed

Before you sign anything

  1. Get medical care, and keep your follow-up appointments.
  2. Keep records: photos, bills, the police report, and notes on how you feel each day.
  3. Don't sign a release or give a recorded statement to the other side's insurance company before you talk to a lawyer.
  4. Send us the offer. A licensed attorney will review it with you.

FAQ

Straight answers about the fee, your attorney, and how your case works.

How much is your fee?

10% of the money you recover. Premium, with in-person service, is 25%.

What if I don't recover anything?

You owe nothing: no fee and no case costs.

Does the fee increase if my case goes to court?

No. It stays 10%.

What are case costs?

Expenses such as medical records, filing fees, and expert reports. We pay them as the case proceeds. If you recover, they are repaid from your share.

Who handles my case?

A licensed attorney in your state. Our software organizes records and drafts documents; the attorney reviews and approves all legal work.

Is this an AI lawyer?

No. AI is a tool our attorneys use. It does not give legal advice or make legal decisions.

Who decides whether to settle?

You do. Your attorney gives you a recommendation and shows what you would keep before you decide.

Do you take small cases?

Yes. A licensed attorney reviews each case before we accept it.

What is my case worth?

It depends on your injuries, treatment, medical bills, the evidence and the insurance available. After reviewing your records, your attorney will explain what affects your case's value and how negotiations usually go, from the opening demand to the final offer.

The insurance company already made me an offer. Is it too late?

Usually not. If you haven't signed a release, send us the offer. A licensed attorney will review it with you before you decide.

I already have a lawyer. Can I switch?

Yes. You can change lawyers at any time. Your current lawyer may be owed part of the fee for work already done. We'll explain how that works before you decide.

Other lawyers turned down my case. Will you look at it?

Yes. A licensed attorney will review it. Cases with complex medical records take a lot of review, and our software helps our attorneys work through those records.

Do I need to visit an office?

No. Everything can be done by phone, online, and WhatsApp. Premium includes in-person meetings.

How do I get updates?

Through WhatsApp and our app, where you can see your case progress, documents, and offers.

How long will my case take?

It depends on your injuries, treatment, and the insurance company. Your attorney will explain the expected timeline for your case.
