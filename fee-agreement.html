Who this agreement is between

This agreement is between [Client name] ("you") and 10flat Law ("the firm").

Your claim

The firm will represent you in your personal injury claim arising from [description of the incident] on [date]. This includes negotiating with insurance companies and, if needed, filing a lawsuit and taking your case to trial.

Note for counselState whether appeals are included, and list anything the firm will not handle, such as property damage or workers' compensation.

Your plan

Choose one:

10flat. The fee is 10% of the recovery. A licensed attorney handles your case by phone, online and WhatsApp.
10flat Premium. The fee is 25% of the recovery. Your attorney personally leads your case and meets with you at a local office.

You can switch from 10flat to Premium at any time by telling the firm in writing.

Note for counselState whether the Premium rate applies to the whole recovery or only to money recovered after the switch, and whether a client can switch back to 10%.

How the fee is calculated

The fee is a percentage of the total recovery, calculated before case costs and medical bills are deducted. "Recovery" means all money paid on your claim, whether by settlement, judgment or award.

  • The percentage does not increase if a lawsuit is filed or your case goes to trial.
  • If there is no recovery, you owe no fee.
Note for counselConfirm the fee is calculated on the gross recovery. If it is calculated after costs, update this section, the example below and the calculator on the homepage.

Case costs

The firm pays case costs as your case moves forward. Examples include fees for medical records and police reports, court filing and service fees, deposition costs and expert reports.

  • If there is a recovery, costs are repaid from your share after the fee is calculated.
  • If there is no recovery, you owe nothing for costs.
Note for counselList any costs the firm will not advance, and whether your approval is needed for costs above [amount].

Medical bills and liens

Some medical providers and health insurers have a right to be repaid from your recovery. These are called liens. The firm will identify them, [try to reduce them], and pay them from your share with your approval.

Note for counselConfirm whether lien negotiation is included in the fee.

Settlement decisions

You decide whether to accept any settlement. The firm will tell you about every offer, show you what you would keep, and give you a recommendation. The firm will not settle your case without your permission.

Updates and communication

You can follow your case, see documents and review offers on WhatsApp and in the firm's app. The attorney responsible for your case is [Attorney name, Bar No.].

What we ask of you

  • Give the firm complete and honest information.
  • Tell the firm about new medical treatment, new bills, or changes to your contact details.
  • Do not discuss your claim with the other side's insurance company without talking to the firm first.

Ending this agreement

You can end this agreement at any time by telling the firm in writing. The firm may withdraw from your case as allowed by the rules of professional conduct.

Note for counselDescribe what, if anything, the firm may be owed if the client ends the agreement before a recovery, as permitted under [State] rules.

Closing statement

When your case ends with a recovery, the firm will give you a written statement before any money is paid out. It will show the total recovery, the fee, each case cost, payments to medical providers, and the amount you receive.

No guarantee

The firm cannot guarantee any result.
